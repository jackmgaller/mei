# Shrine town grey box: the mountain region (rows 4-5, z 256-384)

> **Joined (2026-10-05).** This region's notes from the parallel build. Its test world is gone: the
> level is built only as the world `shrinetown` (`../README.md`), and the generator writes no test
> world; the build commands below that name one are history. What the joining changed is in
> `../DESIGN.md`, section 12.

The north rows of the shrine town as grey boxes: the ridge and the pagoda, the treetop walkway's
north end, the sacred cedar's basin, the fox grove and the torii tunnel, the back mountain with
the stage and the falls, the east shoulder and the bamboo grove. Everything is generated by
`assets/greybox/mountain/make_mountain.py` from the plan's `layout.py`; nothing is hand-edited.

```sh
python3 carts/garden/shrinetown/assets/greybox/mountain/make_mountain.py --layout PATH/TO/layout.py
make B=build-gbm build-gbm/meic build-gbm/mei-headless build-gbm/mei-asset-probe build-gbm/mei-scene-probe
MEIC=$PWD/build-gbm/meic SCENE_PROBE=$PWD/build-gbm/mei-scene-probe \
  python3 tools/mei_world.py build carts/garden/shrinetown/test_mountain.world.json -o build-gbm/shrinetown/mountain \
  --compiler build-gbm/meic --runner build-gbm/mei-headless --probe build-gbm/mei-asset-probe
```

`--layout` defaults to `carts/garden/shrinetown/layout.py` (or `SHRINETOWN_LAYOUT`); layout.py
is not in the repository yet, so pass the plan's copy. Building needs NumPy (the checkers) and
nothing else: the grey boxes have no textures.

## Changes to the plan (layout.py and spec)

1. **Ground under the stage** is `mountain(x, z)`: `height()` returns 60 inside STAGE, which would
   make the stage a solid block instead of a deck on 12 m stilts.
2. **Pagoda tiers.** The plan's walls (half 4.5, narrowing 0.5 a tier) with 1.8 m eaves leave
   0.5 m of each roof outside the eave above: too narrow to stand on and jump past the eave
   (body radius 0.3). Here walls are half 5.0 / 4.25 / 3.5 / 2.75 / 2.0 and eaves half 7.4 / 6.15 /
   5.0 / 3.95 / 2.9, so each roof shows 1.25 / 1.15 / 1.05 / 1.05 m. Roof heights unchanged. The
   footprint at roof 1 is 14.8 m (was 12.6): the south eaves reach z 254.6 (row 3) and the stone
   stair from the north gate arrives under roof 1's SW eave (3.8 m clear). `arch_pagoda_col`
   needs the same exposure.
3. **The kick pair.** layout.py's KICK_CEDAR (178, 253.6) kicks against the pagoda's south wall,
   but roof 1's eave reaches to 1.2 m of its trunk (2.3 m with change 2), closing the shaft at
   23.8. Built as the spec says instead: two cedars face to face 3.2 m apart west of the pagoda,
   here north-south at x 168 (z 259.5 and 264.5). **Connector: drop KICK_CEDAR** (it is in row 3).
   Their crowns stop at 42: the race glide G8 passes over them at 43.9, 0.2 m west of the trunks
   (the spec's 25-41 m cedars would stand in its line).
4. **The knot hole and the rope.** Sill 21.5, 1.4 x 2.2 (the spec), lip 23.7-24.8. The rope runs
   from 25.75 over the rope deck to 23.25, 1.2 m short of the hole: the hands' line, so the feet
   go 24 -> 21.5 as the spec's numbers do (hanging puts the feet 1.75 below the rope). 20.6 m,
   8.2 s at 2.5 m/s. A hang jump (+1.1) and 1.2 m forward enters.
5. **Root door D** on the cedar's west foot, z 294.6-297.4 (layout.py's SHORTCUTS has
   (151, 284) -> (151, 290), south of the trunk; the spec says the west side).
6. **Fox shrine** moved 6 m west to 80-88 x 304-310: the tunnel's first leg runs up x 92-94
   through the plan's 86-94. Statues at (91.2, 298.6) and (96.8, 298.6), flanking the tunnel.
7. **Torii: 50, not about 60**: every 1.4 m, none within 1.6 m of a switchback.
8. **Rope ladder A** runs from the basin floor (13) at (154, 311) to a flat ledge at 44: 31 m,
   10.3 s (spec: 20 m from a slope at 24). The basin's north rim rises 11 m in 6 m there and the
   cliff band above is 40-50 degrees: there is no standing place at 24.
9. **The ledge and the stair through the stilts.** The ledge is a flat at 44 cut into the cliff
   band, running under the deck's front edge and east as a cliff-top path to the falls' lip (the
   back mountain's face, z 316-356, is 40-42 degrees everywhere: not walkable). The spec's stair
   up through the stilts does not fit (the ground meets the deck's underside 10 m back from its
   front edge); it is a 16 m ladder (pole) from the ledge to the deck's front edge.
10. **Kick chimney** moved 8 m south to z 322-331.5 (plan: 330-338, where the ground is already
    41-48, so there is no 16 m foot). Its foot is a new flat at 16 west of the pool (the spec's
    "pool's west terrace"), its top 46 (the blocks' tops), 2 m above the ledge path.
11. **Falls.** The lip is the top of the rock over the cave (48 at z 336); the sheet hangs at
    z 335.4 (plan: 338-342, which is inside the hillside). The cave floor is a flat at 14.
12. **Falls' top** is a flat at 52 (222-236 x 338-350): layout.py's ground there is a 42-degree
    slope. A gully is cut above the lip for the falls' stream (48.4 -> 61), and it is what the rope
    bridge crosses (layout.py's ground under the bridge is level with its ends, 53-54).
13. **Rope bridge** from (223.6, 52, 346) to (169.6, 60, 347): 54 m, 8 m up, as the spec.
14. **Shelf C** (226, 318) set to 20, radius 4.
15. **The bell** at (154, 353.9), under the hall's south eave: the plan's (154, 358) is inside the
    hall (its wall is at z 355). Star 3's coin at (154, 61.2, 353.6).
16. **Hall pad**: the slope behind the deck (60-63) is levelled to 60 under the hall.
17. **Pagoda coins** on the west eaves: the south eaves are in row 3.
18. **Ridge to basin** is stone steps (21.3 -> 13.1 over 18 m), not a trail: the slope is 36-38
    degrees, which the player slides down and cannot climb. The basin-to-falls trail goes round the
    west terrace; steps lead from the pool to the cave (11.2 -> 14) and to the terrace (12.3 -> 16).
19. **Shoulder trail**: two extra points at each switchback ((262, 300) and (296, 322) turned more
    than 120 degrees, which a sweep cannot mitre).
20. **Trees** are kept out of 10 m corridors under G6, G7 and G8 and 12 m round the sacred cedar.
21. **Rims** (spec 1.8: no glide leaves the level): west +14 (a glide from the stage's west end

## Problems and open points

- **The one way into the cedar can be beaten by gliding.** The glider turns (2 rad/s), so a
  glide from any place high enough and near enough can circle off its extra height and come in
  from the west: from roof 2 of the pagoda (29.6 + 3.4) a curved line of about 46 m arrives at
  about 21.4, the sill. The spec's argument counts straight lines only. Test in the playtest;
  fixes: close the glider on touching the lip or the shide streamers, or put the hole in a
  recess that only a hang-jump reaches.
- **The back mountain's face** (z 316-356) is 40-42 degrees across the whole width in layout.py:
  a wall. Every way up is built (torii steps west, shoulder trail east, ladders and chimney in the
  middle), but nothing else can be walked there, and the falls' top needed a flat.
- **The kick pair and G8** are 0.2 m apart; if the race line moves (spec 11, the torii's beam),
  the kick cedars' crowns can grow again.
- **The rims** stop at the row seam; the west rim (+14) runs along the bamboo grove, which the
  spec calls a soft edge to the bamboo level: a rim until that level exists.
- **The path out** (42) at (252, 378) is a marker against the north rim; no door entity (no
  world to go to).
- **Collision cracks**: 21, all small (0.25 m at most), on steep 2 m banks and where sweeps meet
  the ground; same kind as the shrine's 44.
- **Stand-ins**: c2_4 149 and c3_5 143 against the spec's 140 for landmark cells, from the grey
  trees; real trees (4 triangles at range) bring them under.
- The garden's game schema does not list `test_mountain` (a warning only); the test world is
  not in any cart.
