# Shrine town grey box: the core region (z 128–256, cells c*_2 and c*_3)

> **Joined (2026-10-05).** This region's notes from the parallel build. Its test world is gone: the
> level is built only as the world `shrinetown` (`../README.md`), and the generator writes no test
> world; the build commands below that name one are history. What the joining changed is in
> `../DESIGN.md`, section 12.

Notes for the connector. Everything here is generated by
`assets/greybox/core/gen_core.py --layout PATH/layout.py` (default
`carts/garden/shrinetown/layout.py`; layout.py was not copied into the repository, so put it
there or pass the path). Rerun it rather than editing its outputs.

only while `cells/` holds the core cells alone. Entity IDs are `core_*`, path names `core_*`.

## Things built

- Courtyard: the two side halls, six stone lanterns, a chozuya and the shrine office (boxes).
- Precinct: the terrace (cliff, 5.0), the temple podium (cliff, 8.6), the gate, two corridor
  halls, the temple, the bell pavilion, the white wall in 12 runs, the west, east and north gates
  (posts and lintels), three mossy boulders where the wall is easy; stairs (sweeps) up to the
  gate, the temple, out of the west and east gates and the north stone stair.
- Every roof is a stepped stand-in for a walkable roof: a 0.5 m eave slab at the real assets'
  eave height (about 4.2 m above the building's base, so double jump and grab reaches it) and
  then tiers of at most 2.75 m up to layout's ridge, whose top tier is layout's upper roof box.
- Shortcut B: layers `gate_b_barred` (on at start; the bar) and `gate_b_open` (the leaves swung
  in), group `gate_b`.
- West woods: decks 1–5 (9, 12, 15, 18, 21 m) on trunk boxes with crowns, the crown deck (28) with
  its landing at 15 and a 13 m `pole`, rope bridges deck to deck, the bridge from deck 1 down to
  the walkway's foot, the woods trail and the canal lane (draped).
- Pond: water at 0 over layout's bed, 14 stepping stones (top 0.3) along R_WATER, the zig-zag
  bridge from the east gate stair down to the bank, the stream draped downhill into the pond.
- Cemetery: nine terraces (cliffs, 1.8–16.2), 40 grave rows (merged), a stair flight at every
  terrace edge on x 286, the gate at its foot, the jizo hall on top.
- Viaduct: the span (306, 134) → (320, 146) with its pier and both parapets as rails.
- Park: yagura, toilet, jungle gym, swings, a slide tagged `slide`, six ginkgo, the watermill
  (house and wheel), the arched bridge (z 160) and the plank bridge (z 203.5); the park path.
- c0_3: the sake brewery (16.2) and bamboo (scatter).
- Trees: scattered cedars and maples (trunk boxes, canopy boxes; levels: canopy alone from
  36–40 m, a three-sided cone from 80–90 m; thinned from 56 m as the shrine's forests).
- Rails: the four lantern strings (paths with a thin rope sweep and `rail` entities), the
  viaduct parapets over the core span.
- Coins (`coin`, asset `gbc_coin`): 30 — two on each string, the two side halls', two corridor
  halls', the temple's and the gate's ridges, decks 1–5 and the crown deck, five pond stones,
  five cemetery terraces. No star or red coin of layout.py lies in the core rows (★2's torii
  is at z 124, c2_1).

   straddled the retaining wall with nothing for the stair; now it stands on the terrace with the
   stair (z 152–166, 0.9 → 5.0, 12 m wide) in front, as the built shrine's.

   front of the wall, as built): x 115 (was 114), x 213 (was 214), z 233 (was the bulge at 236,

   gate 12.2 / 18.6), but the real assets are lower: side hall ridge 10.3 above its base (10.9),

   (84,232) (96,255.5) instead of R_WEST's (96,150) (84,176) (78,206), which ran through deck 1–3's

## Measurements

peak **1,755 triangles, 378,369 draw CPU cycles, 567,110 GPU cycles**; no view over a budget.

steep banks meet floors; gaps 0.02–0.25 m), none on a seam. Threshold (report only): c3_2's
stand-in 428 triangles over the test world's 400.

  with the core's stand-ins at 93–428 it is about double. Capping stand-ins (spec 8.3 option 1)
  is needed, not optional.

  already helps; the trees are merged per 16 m chunk.
