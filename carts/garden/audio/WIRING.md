# Wiring the garden's sound

The movement garden has no audio yet. This folder holds all of it, made and checked but not
wired in: the shrine town's music and places, the robot's sounds and the goals'. This file is
what the cart needs to play it. The cart's code is the controller lead's (README.md, "Teams");
nothing here changes it.

## What is here

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `sound.akr` | The sound engine: the music sequencer, the zones (bed, music mix, reverb, footsteps, life), emitters, timers, the robot's and the goals' sounds. Hand-written |
| `sound_data.akr`, `sounds.adp` | 60 sounds in one 4-bit ADPCM bank, and the tables: zones, emitters, timers, life. Generated by `gen_sounds.py` |
| `music_data.akr`, `music.adp` | "Momiji", the music: samples and score. Generated by `gen_music.py` |
| `garden_dsp.py` | The DSP helpers both generators import |
| `cart_hooks.akr` | The cart's side, ready to move to `carts/garden/sound_hooks.akr` (below) |
| `demo.akr` | A stand-alone demo cart (below, "Listening") |

What it sounds like, briefly:

- **Music**: "Momiji", D on the yo scale (D E G A B), 75 bpm, A A B A, five layers: koto, a
  bamboo flute, the sho's held clusters, a plucked bass, and a taiko with a wood block and suzu.
  Each layer is a track; the zone's **mix** sets their gains, fading over about two seconds. The
  town plays all five; the shotengai turns them down under the shops' loudspeakers; the
  precinct drops the drums; the woods keep the flute, the sho and a little koto; the mountain the
  flute and the sho; the stage only the sho, under the wind; the cemetery, the culvert and the
  cave a little koto and the sho. Each section has two or three variants, picked at random.
- **Beds** (looped ambience, crossfading over about a second): distant traffic in the town, the
  shopping street's murmur, a breeze and leaves on the precinct's gravel, wind in the cedars,
  mountain wind with gusts, bamboo knocking.
- **Emitters** (positional loops, the nearest two play): the falls (heard from the cedar's basin
  before they are seen), the gorge's stream, the canal, the culvert, the watermill, the
  shotengai's loudspeakers (a tinny shop tune along the arcade), and the train, which moves.
- **Timers at a place**: the shishi-odoshi by the pond (every 9 s or so), the station's
  "pin-pon" chime, someone praying at the temple (the suzu, two claps), and the town's five
  o'clock chime from the loudspeakers, echoing (first at 5 minutes, then every 6).
- **Life** now and then, by zone: crows, higurashi (the evening cicada), a bulbul, a black kite
  over the town, pigeons, a bicycle bell.
- **Reverb by zone**: room in the streets, studio in the covered arcade and the torii tunnel,
  hall in the concourse, the courtyard, the precinct, the woods and the gorge, space in the cave
  behind the falls and the culvert, and echo on the stage (the mountain answering).
- **The robot** (a wind-up tin robot): footsteps on seven materials (stone, gravel, wood,
  earth and leaves, metal, roof tile, water), a spring for the jump (pitched up for the double),
  a wind-up spin for the third jump and the flips, landings, the ground pound, wall kicks, the
  glider's propeller, the rail grind, slides, ledges, poles, bounce, splash, bonk, and the key
  wound when it goes back to the start.
- **Goals**: coins; red coins rising up the scale one note each (D E G A B D E G), the eighth a
  run; the star coming down; the star taken (a koto, flute and bell fanfare, the music ducked
  under it); a shortcut opened; the last train's clock (a tick a second, two in the last ten),
  won and lost; a door; and the stage's temple bell, heard in the whole level.

## The channels

`sound.akr` takes all sixteen channels and the reverb; the cart plays nothing else.

| Channels | Use |
|---|---|
| 0–6 | the music, dry |
| 7, 8 | the zone's bed (two, to crossfade) |
| 9, 10 | the two loudest emitters |
| 11 | the robot's loop (glide, rail grind, wall slide and slides) |
| 12–15 | sounds (`stdlib/voice.akr`): goals 6, robot 3, places 2, footsteps 1, life 0 |

The reverb takes the sounds, the emitters and the timers, not the music or the beds. A zone
with a new preset fades the wet level out, switches, and fades it in (switching clears the
reverb's memory). Nothing reads `RAND`: sound never changes a scenario.

## The wiring, step by step

Every step below was applied to a copy of the cart (the integration branch at 70178b4) and
checked: it compiles, the garden's scenarios (`tests/check.sh`) still pass, and the shrine
town's scenarios play as expected (the WAVs in "Listening").

**1. Move `cart_hooks.akr` to `carts/garden/sound_hooks.akr`** (it imports
`audio/sound.akr` relative to the cart's folder) and import it in `game.akr` after the others:

```
import "hud.akr"
import "sound_hooks.akr"
```

It holds three functions: `sound_tick()` (each tick: the player's state changes, footsteps,
loops, splash, bonk, pole clicks, the clock, the stars coming down), `sound_frame()` (each
frame: the train, its chime and melody, then `ga_frame`) and `sound_world()` (a world opened).

**2. `game.akr`, `init()`**: after `cam_reset()`:

```
    ga_init()
    sound_world()
```

**3. `game.akr`, `update()`**: in the tick loop after `goals_tick()`, `sound_tick()`; after
`cam_place()`, `sound_frame()`. The respawn:

```
    if pressed(Y) && !menu_open {
        player_respawn()
        ga_play(GA_S_WINDUP)
    }
```

**4. `game.akr`, `collect_coins()`**: where a coin is counted:

```
            if ty == RED_COIN_TYPE {
                red_coins += 1
                if red_coins >= red_total { ga_play(GA_S_RED_ALL) } else { ga_red_coin(red_coins) }
            } else {
                coins += 1
                ga_play(GA_S_COIN)
            }
```

**5. `game.akr`, `world_switch()`**: `ga_play(GA_S_DOOR)` first, `sound_world()` after
`cam_reset()`.

**6. `goals.akr`**:
- `goals_collect()`, after `note_star_at = sim_t`: `ga_star_get()`.
- `trig_fire()`, where a challenge is won (`chal_end = 1`): `ga_play(GA_S_WON)`; at its end:
  `if streq(nm, "bell") { ga_bell(wp_entity_pos(e)) } else if p.keep || p.timer > 0 { ga_play(GA_S_SHORTCUT) }`
  (the shortcuts are the kept triggers; the omamori starts the clock).
- `goals_tick()`, where the clock runs out (`chal_end = 2`): `ga_play(GA_S_LOST)`.

That is all the cart needs. Everything else (zones, beds, reverb, emitters, timers, life, the
music) follows from `ga_frame(cam.eye, cam.yaw, pl.pos)`: the ear is the camera, the zone is
the feet's.

### The events, in one table

| Event | Where | Call |
|---|---|---|
| Jump, rollout / double / third, side flip, backflip / long jump | state change (`sound_tick`) | `ga_jump(0 / 1 / 2 / 3)` |
| Landing | air state to a standing one | `ga_land(false, material)` |
| Ground pound: spin, landing | `PoundSpin`, `PoundLand` | `GA_S_POUND_SPIN`, `ga_land(true, material)` |
| Wall kick (and the jump off a pole) | `WallKick` | `GA_S_KICK` |
| Awning bounce | `Bounce` | `GA_S_BOUNCE` |
| Glider out, gliding | `Glide` | `GA_S_GLIDE_OPEN`, loop `GA_S_GLIDE` |
| Wall slide; slide, crouch slide, belly slide | those states | loop `GA_S_WALL_SLIDE` (the slides softer, lower) |
| Rail grind | `Rail` | loop `GA_S_RAIL_GRIND`, pitch with speed |
| Pole, rail or hang grabbed; climbing a pole | `Pole`, `Rail`, `Hang`; every 0.5 m | `GA_S_POLE_GRAB`; `GA_S_POLE_CLIMB` |
| Ledge grab, climb | `LedgeHang`, `LedgeClimb` | `GA_S_LEDGE_GRAB`, `GA_S_LEDGE_CLIMB` |
| Dive, skid, bonk | `Dive`, `Skid`, `pl.bonked` | `GA_S_DIVE`, `GA_S_SKID`, `GA_S_BONK` |
| Into water from the air | `pl_wade` rising while airborne | `GA_S_SPLASH` |
| Footsteps | every tick in `Ground` | `ga_walk(hspeed(), on ground, pl.floor_surf, wading)` |
| Back to the start (Y) | `update()` | `GA_S_WINDUP` |
| Coin, red coin, the last red coin | `collect_coins()` | `GA_S_COIN`, `ga_red_coin(n)`, `GA_S_RED_ALL` |
| A star comes down | `star_drop()` from −1 to above 0 | `ga_star_appear(pos)` |
| A star taken | `goals_collect()` | `ga_star_get()` (ducks the music 4 s) |
| The bell's rope (star 3) | `trig_fire()`, flag `bell` | `ga_bell(pos)`: everywhere in the level |
| A shortcut opened, the omamori taken | `trig_fire()`, kept or timed | `GA_S_SHORTCUT` |
| The clock | each tick | `ga_clock(chal_left)` |
| Challenge won, lost | `trig_fire()`, `goals_tick()` | `GA_S_WON`, `GA_S_LOST` |
| The train | each frame, the mover with start flag `race5` | `ga_emitter_move(GA_EM_TRAIN, pos, live)` |
| The train at the platform; about to leave | `chal_left` 900; 330 | `GA_S_STATION_CHIME`, `GA_S_DEPARTURE` at (160, 12, 8) |
| A door | `world_switch()` | `GA_S_DOOR`, then `ga_world(k)` |

## Zones, reverb and footsteps: what is in the tables, and what the world could add

The zones are boxes in `gen_sounds.py` (`ZONES_TOWN`, after DESIGN.md's sections 2.2 and 3),
in metres with a height range; the first box holding the feet wins, so the cave, the culvert,
the concourse, the platform, the stage, the torii tunnel and the arcade come before the large
areas, and "town roofs" (z < 118, 5.5 m up) makes the roofs tile underfoot. 28 zones in the
shrine town; the garden and the shrine have one each for now (a breeze, pigeons and the town
mix; the woods' bed, the precinct mix and hall). No world change is needed for any of it.

When the world lead wants the zones in the world instead, a `sound_zone` entity (a box: `size`,
and `bed`, `mix`, `reverb`, `wet`, `material`, `life` as names) would replace the table; the
engine's zone lookup (`ga_zone_at`) is the only place that reads them.

**Footsteps.** The material is the floor's surface byte when it names one, else the zone's:

| Byte | Tag | Material | State |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0, 1, 2 | default, `bounce`, `slide` | the zone's | as now |
| 3 | `water` | water | already the shrine town's (`shrinetown.world.json`) |
| 4 | `wood` | wood | proposed: decks, bridges, the stage, the walkway, verandas |
| 5 | `gravel` | gravel | proposed: the courtyard, the precinct, paths |
| 6 | `earth` | earth and leaves | proposed: the woods' floor, trails |
| 7 | `tile` | roof tile | proposed: the kawara roofs |
| 8 | `metal` | metal | proposed: the fire tower, ladders, the overpass, the station canopy |

Bytes 4–8 are a proposal for README.md's "Surfaces" table (new bytes go there first, through
the main session). They behave as 0 for the controller (it only tests 1 and 2), but
`surface_map` has 8 entries, so byte 8 needs it to have 16. Until the world tags its
materials, the zone's material stands in: gravel in the courtyard and the precinct, earth in the
woods, wood on the walkway and the stage, tile on the town's roofs, water in the culvert and
whenever the robot wades.

## What it costs

| | |
|---|---|
| ROM | 1,328,344 bytes: `sounds.adp` 1,084,640 (one-shots 666,704, beds 268,992, loops 148,944), `music.adp` 218,416, code and tables about 25 KB. The garden's ROM is 20.18 MB now, so 21.5 MB with it (+6.6 %; the limit is 64 MB) |
| RAM | 1,212 bytes of globals (the engine's state; everything it plays is read from ROM) |
| CPU | 8,900 cycles a frame on average, 13,400 at most (0.9–1.3 % of the 1,000,000 budget), measured in the plaza (all five music layers, the shop music, the train) and at the falls' pool, with a footstep every 8 frames and a sound every 20 |
| Channels | all 16, and the reverb |

Rates: sounds that need no top end are stored at 7,350 Hz (town and mountain beds), 11,025
(the other beds, the emitters, pigeons, the loudspeakers) or 16,000 Hz (the bell, crows, the
kite, the suzu, the station chime) and played at the matching pitch. Levels were set on the
renders below: the town about −20 dBFS, the woods −24, the music alone −24 in the town, nothing
clipping.

## Listening

The demo cart, and WAVs of it, render with any build (here `build-mine`):

```sh
make B=build-mine build-mine/meic build-mine/mei-headless
build-mine/meic carts/garden/audio/demo.akr -o build-mine/garden-audio-demo.mei
build-mine/mei build-mine/garden-audio-demo.mei       # play it: hold UP, DOWN or LEFT at start
build-mine/mei-headless build-mine/garden-audio-demo.mei --frames 5500 --input 0:1,30:0 --wav robot_and_goals.wav
build-mine/mei-headless build-mine/garden-audio-demo.mei --frames 16000 --input 0:2,30:0 --wav tour.wav
build-mine/mei-headless build-mine/garden-audio-demo.mei --frames 12400 --input 0:4,30:0 --wav music_by_place.wav
python3 carts/garden/audio/gen_sounds.py --preview DIR    # every sound as a WAV
python3 carts/garden/audio/gen_music.py --preview DIR     # every music sample
```

- `robot_and_goals.wav` (92 s): every robot and goal sound once, captioned on screen, over the
  plaza.
- `tour.wav` (4.4 minutes): a listener walks the shrine town: the plaza, the concourse, the
  platform as the train comes in and leaves, the shotengai and its arcade, the courtyard and the
  precinct, the woods and the walkway, the fox grove, up the torii tunnel to the stage (the
  bell, the star coming down), the falls' pool and the cave, the cedar's basin, the pond, the
  culvert, the cemetery, the park and the watermill, the canal, the bamboo, the alleys' roofs,
  and the plaza at five o'clock.
- `music_by_place.wav` (3.4 minutes): the music alone, each mix in turn.

In the game itself: with the wiring above applied to a copy of the cart, any scenario renders
with sound by adding `--wav OUT.wav` to `tests/run.sh` (for example `run.sh 452 4700 out --wav
last_train.wav`: the omamori, the clock, the glide over the shotengai, the train, star 5).

The demo's screen is a map of the zones with the listener, the zone's name, its mix and reverb.
During the tour the d-pad walks freely; A starts the tour again.

## Changing it

Never edit `sound_data.akr`, `music_data.akr` or the `.adp` files: change the generators and
run them (`gen_sounds.py` takes about three minutes, `gen_music.py` one). A new zone, emitter,
timer or life entry is a line in `gen_sounds.py`; a new sound is a function with `@snd(...)`
there, then a `GA_S_` constant appears for it. The music's form, chords and tune are in
`gen_music.py`'s `music()`; the mixes in `gen_sounds.py`'s `MIXES`.
